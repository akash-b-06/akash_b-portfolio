ABOUT THE PROJECT

One place for every journey.

Unified Rail is a mobile ticketing and journey planning concept designed to bring different modes of public transportation into one streamlined experience.

The app brings together reserved and unreserved train tickets, metro tickets, live train tracking, journey planning and metro maps to make everyday travel easier to navigate.
