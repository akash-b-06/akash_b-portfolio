ABOUT THE PROJECT

Making grocery management simpler.

The Virtual Pantry concept helps users keep track of the groceries they already have at home.

It provides alerts for items that are close to expiry, suggests recipes based on available ingredients and allows users to mark items as used or reorder products when they run out.
