ABOUT THE PROJECT

Making gifting more meaningful.

GiftShare is a gift registry concept where hosts can create a wishlist and guests can contribute towards the items instead of purchasing random gifts.

The experience was designed to make group gifting easier, more transparent and more useful for both hosts and guests.
